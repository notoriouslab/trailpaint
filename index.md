## 這是什麼 / What is TrailPaint

TrailPaint 是一個純瀏覽器端的手繪風路線地圖工具，適合登山、單車、旅遊部落客、生態導覽、教育單位、教會 NGO 等使用。所有資料存在瀏覽器本地，不上傳伺服器；支援 PWA 可安裝到手機主畫面像原生 App。

TrailPaint is a browser-only hand-drawn style trail map maker, designed for hikers, cyclists, travel bloggers, ecology guides, educators, and churches/NGOs. All data is stored locally in the browser; PWA-installable on phones, tablets, and desktops.

## 三種建立路線的方式 / Three Ways to Build a Trail

1. **從照片建立（v1.3 新能力）**：拖 20 張照片 → EXIF GPS 自動建景點。支援 iPhone HEIC、Android JPEG。無 GPS 照片歸入「待定位」分組。Nominatim 反向地理編碼自動補地名。
   _From Photos (new in v1.3): drag up to 20 photos; EXIF GPS auto-creates spots. iPhone HEIC and Android JPEG supported._

2. **在地圖上手動建**：搜尋地點、畫路線、放景點卡片。3 步引導教學 + 8 條範例路線試玩。
   _Manually on the Map: search locations, draw routes, place spot cards. 3-step tutorial, 8 sample trails._

3. **用 AI 生成**：複製內建提示詞給 ChatGPT / Claude，描述行程 → 產可匯入的 JSON。
   _Generated by AI: copy prompt template to ChatGPT / Claude; describe your trip; get importable JSON._

## v1.4 Era Slider, Compilations, Postcards, Church Landing (2026-05)

Pushes TrailPaint from "static hand-drawn map" to "spatio-temporal storytelling". Designed for church Sunday school / weekly bulletins / personal devotion, but applies equally to history teaching and cultural routes.

### Era Slider (TimeSlider)

Horizontal pill at the top of the map, oldest left → modern right. Drags cross-fade the historical overlay tile layer; spots whose `era` window doesn't overlap the current year fade to 0.15 opacity (route shape preserved, only contemporary spots stay vivid).

Two scale groups toggle:
- 🗺️ Historical maps (default): 10 ticks aligned with the 9 overlays + modern
- 📖 Biblical narrative: Exodus BC 1446 / King David BC 1000 / Babylonian Exile BC 586 / Jesus AD 30 / Paul AD 50 / Revelation AD 95

Slider auto-filters overlays whose geographic bounds intersect the current spots. A London route shows only "modern + Roman AD 200"; a Taiwan hike shows only the three Taiwan overlays.

### Compilation (multi-story bundles)

`catalog.compilations[]` schema groups multiple story segments into one Player session. URL: `/app/player/?compilation=<id>`.

Two playback modes:
- `sequential` — preserves segment order (e.g., Paul's first → second → Rome)
- `chronological` — sorts spots by `era.start` globally (Zhang Qian BC 138 → Xuanzang AD 629 → Marco Polo AD 1271, mixed across human authors)

Partial-fail tolerant: one failed segment doesn't block the others.

Three default compilations:
- `paul-three-journeys` — 34 spots, AD 46–62, sequential
- `four-gospels-map` — 33 spots, AD 27–30, sequential (Galilean ministry + Transfiguration + Passion Week)
- `silk-road-2000` — 59 spots, BC 138–AD 1295, chronological (cross-author Silk Road dialogue)

### Electronic Postcard (1080×1080 IG square)

Click 📮 on any spot → downloads a PNG postcard with three sections:
- Top half: current map view (auto-zoomed to 10 for low-res overlays / 12 for hi-res Taiwan jm series, capped against overlay's native zoom pyramid so historical tiles stay sharp)
- Middle: spot card (280×280 photo + title + scripture ref + description + project name in italic bottom-right)
- Bottom: era stamp (公元 X 年 / AD X / 西暦 X 年 in three locales) + "via TrailPaint · trailpaint.org" watermark

Smart fallback: if the spot sits outside the active overlay's geographic bounds, the postcard renders against the modern basemap instead (no Taiwan historical map floating over London).

### Church Landing Page (/church/)

Vertical landing for church usage with three iframe-embedded use cases:
- Sunday school: embed Four Gospels Map
- Weekly bulletin: embed Paul's Three Journeys
- Personal devotion: embed Silk Road 2000

Embed code is one-click copy from Player ⚙ menu — paste into WordPress / Wix / Google Slides.

### Spot Era Schema (v5)

`Spot.era?: { start: number; end: number }` — calendar years, BC negative, AD positive, range [-3000, 3000]. Drives postcard era stamp + TimeSlider opacity fade. 17 historical segments / 150 spots already tagged: Paul × 3, Galilean ministry × 2, Passion Week × 3, Zhang Qian × 2, Xuanzang × 2, Marco Polo × 2, Wen Tianxiang × 2, Xu Xiake × 1. Modern stories (Taiwan hikes, 19th-century missionaries) stay era-agnostic intentionally — their spots remain visible at any TimeSlider position.

## v1.8 Site Timeline (2026-10)

A new player layout for one place across eras. The map stays on the place (no fly-to, no popups); dragging the era slider or pressing play lists, in a side panel (below the map on phones), every phase that existed in that year, with full text, sources and photos. Playback steps through the years where a phase appears or disappears. First story: [Da'an Forest Park](https://trailpaint.org/taiwan/taipei-daan-park/) — one Taipei block from the 1904 rice fields and Chaizhuwei hamlet, the 1932 "Park No. 7" plan, military villages and the International House, to the forest park opened in 1994. Only phases with a quoted source are included.

## v1.7 Cumulative Timeline + Taiwan Historical Maps (2026-10)

The era slider gains a cumulative mode: at any year it shows only places that already existed (not yet built or already demolished = hidden), with tick spacing chosen from the story's own event span and playback stopping at each event year. Five Academia Sinica Taiwan maps join the overlays (1904 colonial map, 1945 US Army city plans, 1955, 1989 and 1993 topographic maps); in cumulative mode the basemap switches to the latest map not later than the slider year, and maps with no coverage at the story's location are skipped. First story: [Da'an District, a Century](https://trailpaint.org/taiwan/taipei-daan/) — 24 public facilities in Taipei's Da'an District, 1904 to today, each with its opening year at the current site and sources.

## v1.5–v1.6.5 Editor IA Redesign, Basemap Refresh, Route Hiding (2026-08)

### Editor IA redesign (v1.5)

Cold-start screen with three action cards (Import Photos / Paste AI JSON / Start on the Map). ### Basemap refresh (v1.6)

Default basemap is now Protomaps vector (labels follow UI language: zh-Hant / en / ja, retina-sharp). Lineup: Protomaps Standard / OSM Trails (best mountain-trail detail) / OpenTopoMap / Esri Satellite / Protomaps Dark. Historical-overlay opacity slider now renders inline right under the selected overlay.

### Route hiding (v1.6.5, community contribution)

Routes can be hidden individually from the route list; spots bound to a hidden route hide with it. WYSIWYG: hidden state carries through Player playback, PNG export stats, and GeoJSON export. Single-photo days form a route too (exported as GeoJSON Point per RFC 7946). Hidden state round-trips through share links.

## Privacy and Data

- **Zero backend**: all project data stored in browser localStorage, never uploaded
- **Zero account**: no signup required
- External calls only: map tiles (Protomaps / OSM / OpenTopoMap / Esri), Photon (primary) / Nominatim (fallback) reverse geocoding, Open-Meteo elevation, Cloudflare Workers share-link backend (user-triggered)

## Tech Stack

- Framework: Vite + React 19 + TypeScript 5 (strict)
- Map: Leaflet + react-leaflet + protomaps-leaflet
- State: Zustand + zundo (temporal undo/redo)
- PWA: vite-plugin-pwa + Workbox (active update prompt)
- Import: exifr (EXIF GPS incl. HEIC), @tmcw/togeojson (KML), custom GeoJSON parser
- Export: html-to-image + Canvas post-processing + style filters
- Multi-page build: Editor at /app/, Player at /app/player/
- Story Page: static HTML + vanilla JS (native SEO/OG)
